Leave policy

The entitlement is earned month by month from the accrual rate (1.5 days/month credits 1 day, then 2, so the balance grows through the year) and is capped by the carry-forward cap. Without an assignment the published defaults apply. The monthly job posts each month to the accrual ledger; Accrue now does the same on demand and is safe to press twice.

Anonymise
Irreversible. Name, email, phone, address, date of birth, emergency contact and dependents are erased, and the subject's own audit history is scrubbed. Department, designation, joining date and all payroll / leave / attendance records are kept, because they are statutory.

Permissions

Anything left at its role default is not stored, so the role matrix keeps applying. Tick a box away from the default to store an explicit allow or deny for this user.

Grant leave days to
An adjustment to this year's balance, on top of what the leave policy entitles them to. It is recorded in the grant history and in the audit log with before/after totals, and the employee is notified. A negative value reverses an earlier grant through this same route, so the correction lives in the same ledger as the mistake.
Required and stored with the grant. "HR/Admin added 3 days" is not a fact anyone can audit months later.
Set password for
This signs the employee out everywhere. All their sessions are closed and any sign-in lockout is cleared. The usual reason to reset a password is that it may have been exposed, and a session that survived the reset would be exactly the thing you are trying to end. The action is recorded in the audit log; the password itself is never stored there.
Setting your own password this way needs your current password, so a stolen admin session cannot be turned into permanent ownership of your account. You can also use the change-password form on your profile.
Leave blank and a compliant password is generated and shown once — there is no email server to deliver it, so copy it and hand it over. Checked against the breach corpus and the SRS policy (FR-AUTH-10).
